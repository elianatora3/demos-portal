# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS only. No JavaScript, no framework, no build step (confirmed by the user).

## Users

Prospective and existing patients of Dental Flower Studio, a single-location dental studio in Bulgaria. They come to the site to decide whether to trust this studio with their teeth, see what is offered, and get in touch. The studio covers both health (general dental care) and aesthetics, and does not lean only one way. Primary language: Bulgarian.

## Product Purpose

The studio's marketing website. It should represent the owner, a young woman who leads the studio, and turn a visitor into a booked appointment or enquiry.

## Positioning

From the studio's own copy: the beauty of a smile and its health need regular care and attention. The studio answers every question about the patient's teeth and draws up a treatment plan for each person that meets current standards for both aesthetics and function. The differentiator is personal attention and an individual treatment plan, delivered by a young owner-led studio. It is not a high-volume clinic.

## Standing Preference

The site must read immediately as a dental studio, in the register of an aesthetic dental studio, with her before/after results as the hero proof. The category standard is executed at full craft rather than a conceptual metaphor. Her logo, name and feminine elegance carry the brand.

## Product Principles

0. Her work is the proof. Show real results before making any claim.
1. The person is the studio: warmth and the owner's character come first, not clinical anonymity.
2. Health and beauty carry equal weight. Never sell aesthetics at the expense of care.
3. Be honest and specific. Publish prices once they are supplied, and never invent proof.
4. Calm, not anxious: many visitors are nervous about dentists, so the site should lower the temperature.
5. Booking should be one obvious step away from anywhere on the page.
